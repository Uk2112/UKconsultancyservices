Heat Exchanger Engineering

Evaporator & Condenser Design Consulting

Heat-exchanger design requires both thermal and hydraulic performance to be considered. Tube diameter, rows, fin density, face velocity, refrigerant mass flux and pressure drop all affect the result.

Engineering scope

  • UA-LMTD / effectiveness analysis
  • Air-side heat transfer
  • Refrigerant-side analysis
  • Circuiting review
  • Tube/fin/row evaluation
  • Pressure-drop assessment

Useful project inputs

  • Capacity
  • Refrigerant
  • Air conditions
  • Tube/fin geometry
  • Rows and circuits
  • Allowable pressure drop

Typical deliverables

  • Heat-exchanger sizing basis
  • Thermal-performance framework
  • Pressure-drop assessment
  • Geometry review
