Reliability Engineering & Failure Analysis

Engineering support for validation, test strategy, troubleshooting and root-cause failure analysis to improve product reliability and engineering decisions.

Engineering scope

Reliability work begins with defining the failure or performance requirement, collecting evidence and selecting tests that can distinguish competing failure mechanisms. CONSULTUTK supports structured technical analysis from problem definition through corrective action.

Typical consulting activities

  • Failure-mode and problem-definition reviews
  • Test strategy and validation planning
  • Root-cause failure analysis
  • Thermal, mechanical and system-level troubleshooting
  • Test-data and performance assessment
  • Corrective-action and design-review support
  • Reliability improvement recommendations
  • Fractional engineering leadership and technical reviews

Applications

  • Product validation
  • Prototype failures
  • Field-return investigation
  • Qualification testing
  • Thermal/refrigeration systems
  • Engineering design reviews
