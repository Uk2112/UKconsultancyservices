Manufacturing & Process Engineering

Practical engineering support for design for manufacturing, process improvement, cost optimization, production support and scale-up.

Engineering scope

Manufacturing performance depends on the interaction between product design, process capability, equipment, inspection and cost. CONSULTUTK helps connect design intent with production reality.

  • Manufacturing-process selection and improvement
  • Cycle-time and productivity assessment
  • Cost and manufacturability analysis
  • Process flow and production-support reviews
  • Inspection and quality-control considerations
  • Prototype-to-production transition support
  • Supplier/manufacturing scale-up support

Applications

  • New product introduction
  • Prototype development
  • Production improvement
  • Cost reduction
  • Process troubleshooting
  • Supplier manufacturing
