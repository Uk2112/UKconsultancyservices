Thermal Systems Engineering

Thermal, Refrigeration & HVAC&R Engineering

Engineering consulting for refrigeration systems, heat transfer, HVAC&R and cooling solutions where system performance, energy use and operating conditions must be considered together.

Engineering scope

Thermal and refrigeration systems require coordinated analysis of loads, refrigerant circuit, heat exchangers, airflow, controls and operating conditions. CONSULTUTK provides practical engineering support from requirement definition through design review and validation.

Typical consulting activities

  • Cooling-load and thermal-duty assessment
  • Refrigeration system concept and component selection
  • Heat-transfer and heat-exchanger analysis
  • Evaporator and condenser performance review
  • Expansion-device and refrigerant-circuit assessment
  • Airflow and temperature-distribution considerations
  • Controls, operating envelope and performance review
  • Prototype testing and troubleshooting

Applications

  • Industrial refrigeration
  • HVAC&R systems
  • Cooling equipment
  • Thermal management
  • Environmental chambers
  • Prototype and product development
